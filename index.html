Telemetry Watchdog

A trend detective that checks its own evidence

NASA Space Apps 2026 · Team Ethical Hackers in Space

NASA data from the repository

The page holds no data. It reads the NASA Giovanni CSV files listed in files.json from this repository and calculates everything in your browser. You can also add your own Giovanni file for Los Angeles or New York. Three variables are supported, AOD, NO2 and PM2.5.

    How it is calculated

    method and limits
    • Change. Median of the monthly means of the first 36 months against the median of the last 36 months. The median is used on purpose, so that single smoke months do not decide the number.
    • p value. Daily values become monthly means (at least 5 valid days). Every month is compared with the same month of all other years (seasonal Mann-Kendall test), corrected for the link between neighbouring months (Hirsch-Slack) and for the number of tests (Benjamini-Hochberg). The correction is repeated after every upload.
    • Trend lines. Seasonal Sen slope, once with all valid data and once with the flagged values removed.
    • Flagged values. A value is flagged when its robust z score exceeds 3.5 against the seasonal level (STL on an asinh scale).
    • Uploads. A file you add is analysed in your browser with the same steps. It never leaves your computer.
    • AOD and NO2 describe the whole air column, not the air at ground level. PM2.5 is a model estimate for ground level.
    • A flag marks an unusual value, not a wrong one. Wildfire smoke is real and gets flagged.
    • A straight trend line is a summary. Several series fall fast at first and flatten later.
    • Event cross-check. Unusual means a robust z score above 3.5 against the same calendar month, from median and MAD. This rule is simpler than the flag rule, so its counts differ from the flagged values.
    • AOD is daily and PM2.5 is monthly. A short real event can be diluted in the monthly PM2.5 value, and smoke high in the air raises AOD without reaching the ground. So only in AOD over several days means not confirmed, not wrong. Only a single isolated day next to a normal day is marked as a possible retrieval problem.
    • A match counts as supported by a second source. MERRA-2 uses satellite AOD as an input, so the two are not separate measurements.
    • The region is read from the bounding box in the file header. Los Angeles and New York are recognised.
